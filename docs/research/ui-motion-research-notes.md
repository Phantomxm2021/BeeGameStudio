## Principles and provenance

The following are BGS's methodological interpretations, not an official Nintendo standard or permission to copy an IP's visual assets.

- [Nintendo official UI/UX job introduction](https://www.nintendo.co.jp/jobs/introduction/design/work03.html), recruitment context: UI mediates game/player, participates in experience flow, considers repeated use and communication across ages, and combines usability with entertainment. Apply to the actual player's next decision and interaction cost. Counterexample: a flow document or indefinite polish for every tiny repair adds cost without improving play. It does not specify universal fonts, timing or usability scores.
- [Nintendo official Zelda UI designer interview](https://www.nintendo.co.jp/jobs/keyword/150.html), BOTW/TOTK context: restrained presence still needs memorable necessary information; loading maps and cooking notes connect information to anticipation/delight. Apply to information salience and the game's subject. Counterexample: hiding urgent combat information, adding artificial loading, or inventing a cooking/map feature. These are situated choices, not a minimal-HUD mandate.
- [Nintendo Wire, 2018-08-22 CEDEC summary](https://nintendowire.com/news/2018/08/22/nintendo-talks-about-the-design-of-the-switchs-os-at-cedec-2018/), **secondary reporting**, citing journalist Takashi Mochizuki: short responsive OS animation, fewer actions, visual/audio correspondence and clear icons/text. Original CEDEC slides/talk not obtained. Use as a question about repeated operation cost; do not turn it into a numeric game timing rule, default destructive confirmation or proven audio-sync implementation.
- [MDN Element.animate](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Element/animate), [Animation.cancel](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Animation/cancel), [reduced motion](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/At-rules/@media/prefers-reduced-motion): Web implementation contracts, not art direction or support claims for non-Web engines. [Chrome Page protocol](https://chromedevtools.github.io/devtools-protocol/tot/Page/) describes screenshot/screencast frame capture; capture cadence is not display FPS.
