# Spatial Interaction Principles

1. Make spatial actions understandable and responsive, even when the game is fantastical.
2. Avoid sustained arm elevation and repeated precision gestures without a reason and comfort testing.
3. Place important information within a usable field of view; allow intentional off-axis exploration.
4. Use depth to clarify relationships, not merely to decorate.
5. Choose world, body, or head anchoring for the actual task; test stability and occlusion.
