# Emotional Experience Arc Framework

## 0. Overview

Defines emotional progression across session and lifecycle.

Used to align mechanics with intended player feelings.

---

## 1. First-Session Hook

Must establish:

- Clear fantasy
- Immediate agency
- Quick reward feedback

Emotional goal: Curiosity + Empowerment

---

## 2. Mid-Term Engagement

Introduce:

- Challenge complexity
- Identity reinforcement
- Personal achievement moments

Emotional goal: Mastery + Investment

---

## 3. Long-Term Identity

Support:

- Player expression
- Social recognition
- Meaningful progression

Emotional goal: Ownership + Pride

---

## 4. Emotional Contrast Principle

Avoid monotone emotional tone.

Alternate:

- Tension
- Triumph
- Loss
- Surprise
- Comfort

---

## 5. Emotional Failure Handling

Failure must:

- Teach
- Motivate retry
- Avoid humiliation

Punishment without growth is prohibited.
