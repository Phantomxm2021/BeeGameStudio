# Lighting & Mood Framework

## Purpose
Define lighting philosophy and atmosphere logic.

---

## 1. Global Mood Tone

Choose baseline:

- Warm → Friendly / Party
- Cool → Strategic / Calm
- Neutral → Competitive clarity

Lighting must match gameplay intensity.

---

## 2. Contrast Model

Define:

- Shadow softness
- Highlight intensity
- Post-processing strength

Avoid extreme contrast that hides gameplay clarity.

---

## 3. Readability Priority

Player > Enemy > Interactive > Environment

Never allow lighting to reduce gameplay clarity.

---

## 4. Platform Constraints

Mobile:
- Avoid heavy real-time shadows
- Prefer baked lighting

PC:
- Real-time shadows allowed
- Higher reflection quality

XR:
- Avoid excessive bloom
- Preserve depth cues
