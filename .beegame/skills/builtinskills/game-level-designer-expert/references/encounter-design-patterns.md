# Encounter Pacing Framework

## 0. Overview

Defines structural patterns for moment-to-moment gameplay tension.

Applicable to combat, puzzle, and challenge encounters.

---

## 1. Encounter Structure Model

Each encounter must include:

1. Setup
2. Escalation
3. Peak
4. Resolution

Flat intensity is forbidden.

---

## 2. Tension Rhythm

Alternate between:

- Safe Zone
- Rising Threat
- High Pressure
- Relief

Avoid sustained maximum intensity.

---

## 3. Skill Expression Design

Encounters must test:

- Previously learned mechanics
- Combination of mechanics
- Player decision-making

Avoid introducing new mechanics during peak stress.

---

## 4. Difficulty Ramp Strategy

Early:
- Clear telegraphing
- Forgiving margins

Mid:
- Multi-threat scenarios

Late:
- Optimization challenges

---

## 5. Spatial Considerations

Define:

- Player movement freedom
- Environmental hazards
- Line-of-sight dynamics
- Escape routes

Avoid cluttered arenas without flow.
