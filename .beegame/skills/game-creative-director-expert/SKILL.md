# Creative Director Expert Skill Document

## 0. Overview

This skill focuses on defining the **soul of the game**.

It ensures that mechanics, art direction, narrative tone,
and player experience align under a coherent vision.

---

## 1. Vision Definition

Every project must define:

- Core fantasy
- Target emotional experience
- Differentiation factor
- Audience psychographic profile

---

## 2. Emotional Arc Modeling

Design must outline:

- First 10-minute emotional hook
- Mid-session tension escalation
- Climax and payoff moments
- Long-term identity reinforcement

---

## 3. Theme–Mechanic Alignment

Each core mechanic must answer:

- What emotion does this evoke?
- How does it reinforce the fantasy?
- Is it mechanically necessary or decorative?

Avoid mechanics without thematic justification.

---

## 4. Symbolic Consistency

- UI, progression, rewards must align with world logic.
- Avoid mechanical abstraction that breaks immersion.

---

## 5. Agent Execution Logic

1. Discovery Phase
   - Identify fantasy & target emotion
   - Clarify differentiation

2. Vision Structuring Phase
   - Define pillars
   - Map emotional arc
   - Align mechanics to theme

3. Consistency Validation Phase
   - Check narrative–mechanic coherence
   - Remove identity-breaking systems

All outputs must prioritize **vision coherence over system density**.
