# Game Creative Discovery

Resolve the current creative question with reference evidence, not a finished direction or a separate research assignment by default. Start from confirmed play, player intent and retained material. Identify what remains unknown about the experience's expression and how resolving it could change the design possibilities. Scene, UI, UX, motion, feel and sound may contribute to the same question; they are not separate investigations to complete. Readability and device requirements constrain a useful expression without becoming the entire creative question.

## Find and interpret evidence

Choose material for the relationship being investigated. A game, animation, film, illustration, physical object or other source may reveal useful spatial, material, interactive or rhythmic behavior. Look beyond genre and style labels: explain what the source actually does and why it produces the relevant effect. Comparable player actions help assess transfer; an unrelated discipline can supply an original connection without proving its suitability for this game.

Use professional methods for the specific question:
- [Art direction](../../skills/game-art-director-expert/SKILL.md) for composition, shape, material and visual relationships.
- [UI/UX](../../skills/game-ui-ux-designer/SKILL.md) for information, navigation and transitions in the player's situation.
- [Game feel](../../skills/game-feel-designer/SKILL.md) for perceived response, action rhythm and coordinated feedback.
- [Audio](../../skills/game-audio-designer/SKILL.md) for audible atmosphere, musical identity and event relationships.

These methods support analysis of the chosen material, not a requirement to read every specialty, use its example works, produce assets or validate an unbuilt game. Retain the original source and useful material paths. Distinguish directly observed features, source-reported explanations, transferable principles and your own creative associations. Match claims to the material: stills establish captured relationships, not motion or sound. Preserve the conditions and uncertainty rather than collecting extra evidence merely to eliminate every limitation. Reference access does not grant asset reuse rights.

## Integrate findings

When delegating, assign a concrete unresolved question with its relevant constraints and retained material. Integrate returned findings with their sources and existing files. Further investigation addresses a specific conflicting observation or missing relationship; it does not repeat the delegated study to certify it.

Synthesize relationships that could support different expressions, their relevance to this game and their limits. Supported comparisons and creative suggestions are useful, but they are not an adopted palette, layout, control mapping, production technique or experiment checklist. Creative Direction develops and compares the candidates. Detailed rules, numerical tuning, asset production and routine device adaptation belong to their subsequent work. Investigate a feasibility issue here when leaving it open prevents a viable creative direction from being developed or compared; otherwise retain it for its owner.

Evidence is sufficient when the current question can inform the candidate expressions and remaining uncertainty can be carried into design. Return the relevant observation, principle, source and limits to the owning design decision or assigned task; keep adopted material at its existing paths. Preserve useful sources and findings on continuation rather than restarting the study.
