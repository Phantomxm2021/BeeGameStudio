# Player Observation

Read this reference when authorized access to representative players exists or an experience-driven change needs player evidence.

Observe a representative task without explaining the solution first. Separate prior familiarity from what the game teaches. Record relevant behavior such as hesitation, repeated failed attempts, recovery and misunderstood cues; ask neutral follow-up questions to distinguish an observation from its possible cause. Avoid leading prompts and treat sample limitations explicitly. Preserve privacy and do not recruit, contact people or collect personal data without authorization.
