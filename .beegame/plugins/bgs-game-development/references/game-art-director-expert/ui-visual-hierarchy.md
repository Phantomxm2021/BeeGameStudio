# Screen Composition Studies

Use to study how existing pictures establish appearance and information hierarchy, or to inform a composition choice or diagnose a visible loss of treatment. A narrow text, binding or input fix can use the established project design without external research. Read the applicable example and picture, not every style at task startup.

## Read the relevant picture

A style name leaves appearance choices open. Compare actual pictures by the player situation, viewing distance, subject proportions, contour, material, typography and distribution of emphasis. Identify the relationship useful to this game and what does not fit. Once the project has a selected design, use it for production and runtime comparison; these examples help explain a choice rather than replace that target.

The official image links below are visual references, not bundled runtime assets or permission to copy artwork, fonts or branding. For a first-hand observation, inspect the relevant image; its title or this prose is not image inspection. When integrating another researcher’s source-backed observations, retain their source, material path, viewing conditions and limits. Inspect a related picture to resolve an identified evidence gap, conflict or new design question; attribute retained observations without claiming to have personally made them. If evidence is unavailable, retain that uncertainty. The observations are BGS's interpretation, not measured player results or universal style definitions.

## Finished-game references

Choose by the unresolved visual relationship: map abstraction (Mini Metro), layered landscape (Alto), dense combat and HUD (Hades), lit faceted 3D space (Deep Rock Galactic), monochrome first-person detail (Obra Dinn), outlined subjects over painted surfaces (Cuphead), or expressive type (Pentiment). These are starting points, not a style menu or required reading set; use an appropriate project/source picture when none answers the question.

### Mini Metro: abstraction that carries information

[Official game page and screenshot gallery](https://dinopoloclub.com/games/mini-metro/).

![Mini Metro official gameplay screenshot: colored routes, outlined stations and waiting passenger symbols](https://dinopoloclub.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/screenshot-2.jpg)

The route colors, dark station outlines and smaller passenger symbols have different jobs and remain distinguishable against the pale map. Repeated line widths, bends and symbol proportions make a coherent graphic family. The water occupies substantial space without competing with those signals. Its geometric abstraction serves a route-planning task; matching its few colors without preserving route continuity and symbol hierarchy would miss the design. The image does not establish its renderer or prescribe a map layout, small HUD or geometric subjects for another game.

### Alto's Adventure: restrained shapes with depth and atmosphere

[Official game page and screenshot gallery](https://altosadventure.com/).

![Alto's Adventure official Dawn gameplay screenshot: a dark slope and rider against layered warm mountain ranges](https://altosadventure.com/resources/screenshots/2_Dawn.png)

The dawn picture uses related warm colors but separates near terrain, distant mountains and sky by value and atmospheric layering. Slope silhouettes, clustered trees, light and haze retain a sense of place despite reduced detail. The rider is small relative to the landscape in this particular view; that proportion serves this composition and is not a default actor size. Flattening all depth ranges into one fill could retain the palette while losing the intended spatial reading. A still cannot establish movement readability or responsiveness.

### Hades: dense painting, combat emphasis and distributed HUD

[Developer game page](https://www.supergiantgames.com/games/hades/) and [publisher Steam gallery](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1145360/Hades/).

![Publisher Hades screenshot, marked v0.23698: bright melee arc in a richly painted room, boss bar above and player health below](https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/1145360/ss_c0fed447426b69981cf1721756acf75369801b31.1920x1080.jpg?t=1758127023)

In this published boss-combat picture, the bright attack arc and warm actors separate from patterned, darker floor planes. The boss bar, left-side abilities and lower health display have distinct positions and scales while the central fight remains exposed. Detail and saturated color coexist with hierarchy; a quiet scene or detached menu panel is not a prerequisite. This early-version still supports those visible relationships, not current-release UI coverage, attack timing or a claim about its renderer. Reuse a placement only when the player's decisions and viewing conditions warrant it.

### Deep Rock Galactic: faceted 3D space with localized light

[Developer game page](https://www.deeprockgalactic.com/).

![Official DRG promotional scene: armed dwarves and insects among faceted cave surfaces, bright weapons and darker distant recesses](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5925832e03596efb6d4b502a/1588156068109-SCYSIX6LE6W1UH657PEM/DRG_Screenshot_001.png)

The published scene separates angular terrain, bulky equipment and creatures through silhouette, overlap and localized illumination. Warm weapon effects compete against cooler rock and dark recesses; faceting alone does not establish the material or action. This promotional camera has no gameplay HUD and is not evidence for first-person framing, effect readability during play or the exact lighting/shader implementation. Use it for a concrete form/light question, then reconcile with the project's actual camera.

### Return of the Obra Dinn: detailed space through limited values

[Developer game page and gallery](https://obradinn.com/).

![Official Obra Dinn ship-deck screenshot: two-tone outlines, stippled surfaces, rigging and receding deck structure](https://obradinn.com/img/shots/Ship-01.png)

The deck picture retains dense rigging, surface marks and perspective depth within a two-tone treatment. Large light/dark masses, contours and stippled regions have different jobs. Limited color does not imply sparse geometry or flat fills. A proposed rendering method might use contour extraction or value quantization/dithering; the picture does not establish the game's algorithm. Those methods must retain the project's important edges and surface identity at play scale; a still cannot establish stability as the camera moves. Use [material and shading](material-and-shading.md) when translating an adopted surface treatment into a renderer.

### Cuphead: distinct marks for actors and surroundings

[Studio MDHR's official press kit](https://studiomdhr.com/press-kits/) identifies hand-drawn cel animation and watercolor backgrounds as production techniques.

![Official Cuphead Cagney Carnation screenshot: dark actor outlines and saturated flower petals over softer painted scenery](https://studiomdhr.com/site/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/cuphead_screenshot_featured_1.png)

The flower, player characters and platforms have firm contours and relatively bounded color areas; the trees, sky and ground carry softer variations and brush-like texture. Dark foreground vegetation adds another depth layer. These different mark systems coexist rather than applying one noise texture or outline to everything. For a selected handmade direction, distinguish the marks that define moving subjects from those describing background material. An outline filter and warm palette alone would miss that construction. This published still shows appearance, not timing, input feel or a requirement to reproduce cel animation in every game.

### Pentiment: typography participates in the art

[Obsidian's game page](https://pentiment.obsidian.net/) connects its expression to manuscripts and printed woodcuts. Microsoft's [published font comparison](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2022/10/13/xbox-accessibility-showcase-2022/) shows its stylized and easy-read treatments:

![Microsoft-published Pentiment comparison: different script forms and easier-read alternatives within the same illustrated paper frame](https://xboxwire.thesourcemediaassets.com/sites/2/2022/10/Easy-Read-Fonts-in-Pentiment-21a07f383ed1d4b980df.jpg)

The illustrated frame remains coherent while letterforms change; stylistic identity need not depend on making all text equally ornate. For a typography question, relate glyph shape, weight, spacing and writing treatment to the selected visual basis, then judge the project's actual words and numerals at display size. [XAG 101](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/101) explains measuring rendered text and the effects of typeface, spacing and viewing conditions. These Latin-script examples do not establish another language's glyph coverage, prescribe historical type, or require an extra font-setting feature in every game. A still cannot verify writing animation or reading comfort.

The restrained and detailed examples answer different visual questions. No example prescribes a palette size, sparse scene or fixed composition. [NN/G's minimalism study](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/characteristics-minimalism/) distinguishes minimalist content/layout strategy from flat graphic treatment; its website findings are not game-art specifications.

## Visual contrasts and degradation

[Riot's Clarity in League](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/clarity-in-league/) includes a real development comparison for Shan Hai Scrolls Jhin. The developers identify borders that faded while the ability opened and explain the change to keep them visible.

![Riot's official Jhin development comparison: ability-border visibility during the opening sequence](https://cmsassets.rgpub.io/sanity/images/dsfx7636/news_live/e3172fe43009612f374c9bc5b0f1040835983c70-1280x720.jpg)

The useful contrast is a local relationship between a boundary, its background and the moment it must be read. The screenshots differ in framing and timing, so they do not establish that an entire composition is better, or that brighter effects are universally preferable. The developer's temporal claim needs the accompanying explanation; a still alone cannot verify it. This is a development defect/correction, not two interchangeable style options.

Use such comparisons to describe what was lost, rather than answer only whether a picture has the same theme or palette:

| Relationship under discussion | A concrete loss to look for in the project's actual pictures | What remains a choice |
|---|---|---|
| Subject identity and proportion | A recognizable silhouette or opening becomes a generic shape; its apparent mass changes relative to its neighbors | Abstraction and reduced ornament can work when they retain the chosen subject treatment |
| Depth and material | Layers merge, overlap no longer explains containment/contact, or characteristic edges and surface cues disappear | Flatness, texture and lighting are project choices; extra detail is not automatically an improvement |
| World/UI hierarchy | A focal subject shrinks behind a panel, or typography, borders and decoration compete equally | A large panel or prominent HUD may be right for a different player situation |

The table describes possible losses, not altered versions of the reference games or observed defects in them. For an established project, compare the corresponding selected picture and actual resource/runtime state at the intended display size. An authorized adaptation may preserve the relationship; a lost relationship remains an implementation problem even when the style label still fits.
