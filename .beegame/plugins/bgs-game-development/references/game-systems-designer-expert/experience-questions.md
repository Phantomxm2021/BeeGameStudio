# Design judgment from a player question

Read for an assigned design critique, proposed mechanic revision or a concrete agency, repetition or challenge problem. Ordinary GDD completion does not require this analysis or a new report.

Input: the authorized player promise, current rule/loop, relevant state and any real observations. If only a concept exists, propose a test; do not invent play evidence.

Describe what the player notices, attempts, changes and learns. Identify a useful choice or skill when that serves this genre; expressive building, discovery, narrative interpretation and contemplative puzzles need not copy action-game risk/reward. A counter or extra feature does not establish a meaningful loop.

Turn the main uncertainty into a falsifiable behavior hypothesis with the relevant starting knowledge and conditions. Name what observation would contradict it, not an invented satisfaction score. Keep the intent, hypothesis and principal risk with the existing owning GDD decision or requested critique. A stated goal does not justify adding progression, rewards or mechanics outside authorization.

Offer the smallest change addressing a supported cause, its tradeoff and the next observation. Separate recorded behavior from a plausible explanation; check competing explanations such as an input defect before redesigning a mechanic. An Agent's strategy is not representative-player evidence. Stop after the requested decision/test question; no full design rewrite or autonomous evaluation loop.

For an authorized runtime check, the existing game-playtest-performance method owns execution and evidence. Route a concrete composition, UI, feel, control or pacing issue to its existing specialist, selecting only what the cause needs.
