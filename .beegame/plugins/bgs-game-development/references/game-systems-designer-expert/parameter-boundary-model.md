# Parameter analysis and tuning

Use for an assigned quantitative design, implementation or tuning question. Trace each value to its source; distinguish requirements, design choices and estimates. A provisional estimate is not a confirmed requirement.

## Define the relationship

Give consequential values their meaning, unit (or dimensionless meaning), scope and dependencies. Keep governing product-rule relationships in the design and runtime/tuning values with their actual code/data or resource configuration, referencing definitions rather than copying tables. Hard legal bounds differ from tentative tuning ranges. Give bounds when invalid/extreme values change rules or address a demonstrated risk.

## Support the claim

Identify whether the claim needs an estimate under assumptions, a proven bound, a legal worked sequence or runtime observation. Use the available Shell for the smallest relevant calculation from actual rules, values, units, states and clocks. Trace worked sequences through legal actions and states; evaluate reachable boundaries that could change the conclusion.

An optimistic duration below a deadline does not prove a feasible route. A simplified estimate is not a bound unless omitted effects preserve its direction. One legal sequence proves that sequence, not an optimum, universal safety or player success rate. Qualify/remove unsupported claims when inputs are unresolved, preserving the underlying requirement and evidence gap. Keep a short derivation and its limits with the owning source. Valid results need no general re-review, new simulator or calculation appendix; recalculate only when substantive inputs change.

## Tune the diagnosed cause

Distinguish numerical causes from control, information or level defects. Change the smallest relevant parameter/relationship and observe affected situations, including a strong strategy/boundary when it could invalidate the change. Separate release values from optional ranges and measured outcomes from targets. PvP and PvE need not share a baseline. Fairness, pacing and fun remain hypotheses until observed.

Use [growth examples](scaling-curve-models.md) for an unresolved baseline/growth relationship and [economy methods](economy-balancing-spec.md) for actual earnable/spendable resources. Do not invent a curve, sink, cap or dominance threshold to use a method.
