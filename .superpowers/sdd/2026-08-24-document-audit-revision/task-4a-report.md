## Fix round 1 corrections

- The schema fingerprint failure was introduced by the Task 4A state cutover; it is now closed by bumping `DELIVERY_RUN_SCHEMA_VERSION` from 29 to 30, registering the measured fingerprint, and replacing literal version-29 fixtures with the shared constant.
- `FoundationFact.evidence` is required end-to-end. Deterministic checker admission validates exact evidence shape, owner SectionRef identity, and absolute line bounds.
